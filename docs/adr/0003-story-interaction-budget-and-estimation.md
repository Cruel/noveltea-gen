# Story interaction, production budget, and estimation ownership

Status: accepted; author-approved baseline/orchestration design, recorded before spec publication.

A new Story's approved Creative Direction must establish a compact **interaction profile** (Choice frequency, branching depth, adventure and separately custom-interactive Puzzle emphasis, degree of content divergence), a target **single-route first-playthrough length**, broad Narrative-versus-Exploration pacing, and production constraints for distinct illustrated backgrounds/variants and Main/Supporting/Extra Character art. User-supplied hard limits and soft preferences are distinguished and protected; suggested defaults and delegated choices still require exact Foundation approval. The orchestrator and architecture specialist translate these Story-wide commitments into suitable distributions without mandatory per-Milestone Choices/Puzzles, fixed Acts, uniform route convergence, or premature engine Room design. Shared core Characters and narrative locations are the default for scope efficiency, with story-justified route-exclusive exceptions.

**Keep enduring requirements and local estimates on relevant canonical records**, not in a separate Git-tracked planning record: Creative Direction owns Story-wide targets, Milestones own broad Narrative/Exploration duration ranges and material route variants, Characters own artwork tiers, and Puzzles distinguish adventure from custom-interactive mechanics. The schema and native NovelTea CLI are the single authority for validating these typed records and deriving source-bound, read-only route duration and distinct-content/illustration footprint metrics. The CLI computes from supplied estimates; it does not decide plausible pacing, creative quality, or developer effort. NovelTea Gen's controller owns approved plan amendments, verified specialist assignments, recovery, and effort-weighted Generation progress; specialists and independent review judge whether the Story actually fulfills its promised player experience. Playtime coverage and generation completion remain separate measures.

An extensive branching Story may require far more distinct content than its single-playthrough length implies. Compare route-aware projected production footprint with approved budgets and show scope warnings; hard limits require redesign or explicit approval, and material soft-budget changes require author review. Choice consequences can persist through shared later scenes, and convergence at chapter/Act boundaries is an optional technique rather than a prescribed structure. Re-estimate at verified source boundaries; propagate uncertainty and disclose changes to estimates and percentages. Do not overload ordinary hot-path CLI validation with optional planning metrics or replace the future Story-to-engine translation layer.

The existing [goal-driven orchestration ADR](0002-goal-driven-story-orchestration.md) remains authoritative: the orchestrator proposes conditional and revisable assignments but cannot create write grants, fabricate skipped work, erase protected decisions, or bypass independent review. The concrete migration sequence and acceptance tests are in [the implementation plan](../story-generation-planning-design.md) and [the implementation specification](../story-generation-production-spec.md).
